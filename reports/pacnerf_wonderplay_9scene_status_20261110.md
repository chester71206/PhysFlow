## Gradient limitation

The material-to-MPM segment and full loss direction checks support training for the stagewise cases, but the renderer derivative magnitude is still unresolved for Droplet, Playdoh, and Toothpaste. The existing Toothpaste count-dependent error is evidence of overlap or visibility sensitivity; it does not prove genuine nonsmoothness or exclude an implementation bug. The 50-update runs for those scenes must retain this qualification even if loss falls.

Additional Toothpaste diagnostics: with only the first frame pair, full-loss yield-stress FD agrees within 2.90%; E gives 14.37% with a loss difference of only five float32 ULPs. With five pairs, full-loss E differs by 38.57%, while the material-to-MPM E segment differs by 9.16%. The five-pair viscosity MPM segment differs by 44.58% at 0.5% FD step, but fixed-cotangent probes differ by 4.29% at 1% and 9.03% at 2%; the small-step FD estimate is unstable. Evidence: `toothpaste_pair1_diagnostic_ds1.0_ep1/material_ad_check.json`, `toothpaste_pair5_diagnostic_ds1.0_ep1/material_ad_check.json`, and `toothpaste_pair5_viscosity_probe_ds1.0_ep1/fd_probe.json` under the output root. This does not establish full renderer AD correctness.

For five-pair viscosity, full image-loss FD at 1%, 2%, and 5% steps differs from AD by 1.43%, 9.31%, and 3.79%, respectively (`toothpaste_pair5_viscosity_full_probe_ds1.0_ep1/fd_probe.json`). Thus the 0.5% viscosity discrepancy is a poor local FD stencil for that interval; it is not evidence by itself of a viscosity reverse-AD implementation error. Five-pair E remains under multi-step investigation.

The five-pair E full-loss multi-step probe (`toothpaste_pair5_E_full_probe_ds1.0_ep1/fd_probe.json`) gives AD/FD errors of 35.64%, 38.57%, 18.58%, 23.21%, and 22.50% at 0.2%, 0.5%, 1%, 2%, and 5% parameter steps. Its discrepancy persists across reasonable steps, so this is not resolved as a single bad FD stencil. The five-pair MPM E segment is within 10%; the remaining mismatch is concentrated in the renderer/objective segment, subject to visibility and overlap diagnostics.

An all-frame Toothpaste yield-stress state-direction probe (`toothpaste_all_frame_position_probe_gpu8_ds1.0_ep1/fd_probe.json`) perturbs one MPM output frame's positions at a time while leaving other states fixed. AD minus FD is about -24.60 for frame 8 and -44.85 for frame 9; most other frames differ by less than 8. The sum of per-frame AD terms is -130.95, versus -47.68 from separate per-frame finite differences. This localizes most of the positions discrepancy to neighboring late frames; it does not yet distinguish a rasterizer backward error from a visibility/depth-order switch. An earlier attempt on GPU 0 exited at Taichi initialization because unrelated GPU allocations filled that device; the GPU 8 retry completed.

A pair-specific follow-up (`toothpaste_pair_position_probe_retry_ds1.0_ep1/fd_probe.json`) isolates the two late source frames. For pair 8, source-frame 8 position derivative is AD -389.10 versus FD -151.40, while its target-frame 9 is AD 1794.16 versus FD 1794.10. For pair 9, source-frame 9 is AD -907.53 versus FD -457.76, while target-frame 10 is AD 1823.88 versus FD 1826.29. The target-only paths through projected displacement agree closely; the source-position paths through rasterized location/visibility account for the discrepancy. This narrows the fault to the renderer's source-position derivative or genuine source visibility/depth-order discontinuities, rather than the projected-displacement derivative. The first pair-specific attempt had a diagnostic-only covariance reshape error and was rerun successfully; training code was unaffected.

The fixed-direction multi-step test (`toothpaste_pair_fixed_direction_probe_ds1.0_ep1/fd_probe.json`) holds the *same MPM state direction* and changes only the renderer perturbation multiplier (0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10). Pair 8 target-frame 9 FD stays 1794.1–1795.3 versus AD 1794.16; pair 9 target-frame 10 stays 1816.7–1826.3 versus AD 1823.88. In contrast, pair 8 source-frame 8 FD ranges from -524.5 to -151.4 versus AD -389.1, and pair 9 source-frame 9 FD ranges from +52.5 to -2766.6 versus AD -907.5. The source objective is demonstrably non-smooth or numerically discontinuous along this fixed direction at these perturbation sizes. This is specific evidence for visibility/contributor/depth-order sensitivity, not a relaxed gradient threshold; a separate backward implementation error remains possible. The full renderer derivative therefore remains unverified.

Playdoh E was split similarly (`playdoh_renderer_state_probe_ds1.0_ep1/fd_probe.json`). Material-to-MPM AD/FD differs by 0.54%; the renderer state-direction differs by 15.66% at the full 19,527 Gaussian sample. At 993, 4,882, and 9,764 Gaussians the same check differs by 5.10%, 1.26%, and 0.99%. The full-sample mismatch is in positions (AD -13.17 versus FD -11.22), while covariance agrees (AD 0.504 versus FD 0.515). Per-frame position checks localize the main difference to output frame 9 (AD +1.15 versus FD +3.13); other material-significant frames agree closely. The full renderer derivative remains unverified.

The completed Playdoh fixed-direction pair test (`playdoh_pair_fixed_direction_probe_ds1.0_ep1/fd_probe.json`) isolates pair 9 source-frame 9: AD +0.281 versus FD from +2.10 to +26.02 as the renderer-only perturbation ratio changes from 0.2 to 1.0. Pair 8 target-frame 9 stays AD +14.644 versus FD +14.59 to +14.69; pair 9 target-frame 10 stays AD +59.098 versus FD +58.75 to +59.32. As in Toothpaste, target/projected-displacement derivatives are locally stable while the source visibility/location path is not. This is direct evidence of genuine source-path non-smoothness or numerical discontinuity for Playdoh at full sample count; it does not prove every rasterizer backward term is correct.
